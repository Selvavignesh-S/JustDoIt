JustDoIt privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

JustDoIt is a task manager for students. This page explains what it stores, why, and who else handles it.

What we store

  • Your account: full name, username, a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself), and your email if you add one.
  • Your tasks: title, category, estimated hours, deadline, description, status and priority.
  • Your files: the files you attach and their older versions.
  • Your phone's notification address: a push token, only if you allow notifications, so reminders can reach you.

The app keeps only your sign-in token on the phone. Everything else stays on the server.

Why

Only to run the app: to show your tasks, work out their priority, remind you before deadlines, and keep your files. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Who else handles your data

  • An AI service: when you use "Describe with AI", only the description you typed is sent, to suggest a title, category and hours. Your deadline, name and account are not sent. You can skip AI and fill in a task by hand.
  • Hosting and storage: the server, database and file storage run with cloud providers in the European Union (Frankfurt). Files are stored privately; each download is checked against your account.
  • Notifications: reminders go through Expo's push service and Google Firebase (Android) or Apple (iPhone). A notification carries the task's title and how soon it is due.
  • Email: if you add an email, it is used only for confirming it and for password reset.

Deleting your data

Deleting a task deletes its files and all their versions. Removing a file deletes all of its versions. Logging out stops notifications to that phone. To delete your whole account, open Settings, tap Delete account and enter your password: your account, all your tasks, every uploaded file and version, and your phones' reminder registrations are deleted permanently, straight away. This cannot be undone.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as BCrypt hashes, and each account can reach only its own tasks and files.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
